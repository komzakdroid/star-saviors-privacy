🗑️ Delete Your Account & Data — Star Saviors

Last updated: 2026-05-30

App: Star Saviors: Cosmic Quest  ·  Developer: Komiljon Zokirov

This page explains how to request deletion of your Star Saviors account and the data associated with it. You created an account by signing in with Google or Google Play Games; this lets your game progress sync to the cloud.

Option 1 — Request by email (available now)

Send an email to:

📧 komzak080@gmail.com

In the email, include:

  1. Subject: "Delete my Star Saviors account"
  2. The Google account email you signed in with (so we can locate your data)

We will delete your account and associated cloud data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Option 2 — In-app (Settings → Delete Account)

From inside the game: open Settings → Sign Out → Delete Account. This removes your cloud-saved data immediately. (If this option is not yet available in your installed version, please use Option 1 above — it is updated in a future release.)

What gets deleted

Delete data without deleting your account

If you want to erase your cloud progress but keep the ability to sign in again, mention that in your email (Option 1). We will wipe your saved data while keeping your account record empty.
